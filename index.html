Frequently asked

Is this a real navigation app?

No. Full Scale is a training aid only. Its stations, fixes and geometry are placeholders on a flat training grid, not survey data. Never use it for real-world navigation — always fly from current official publications.

What is included in the free version?

The full HSI and map sandbox — drag the aircraft, stations and fixes, add wind, fly intercepts — plus one exercise in each category: a radial intercept, a DME arc, and a holding entry.

What does Full Scale Pro unlock?

  • Every exercise in the library, plus everything added in future updates
  • Publishing your own holding patterns and DME arcs
  • Approach-chart PDF overlays on the map
  • Editing the navaid database — add navaids, fixes and marker beacons

Pro is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no account.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open any locked feature to bring up the unlock screen, then tap Restore purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple ID that bought Pro.

My imported chart disappeared after relaunch.

Known limitation: chart overlays are not yet saved between sessions. Re-import the PDF for now — persistence for charts is planned.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

This policy covers the Full Scale app for iPhone and iPad, published by the developer contactable at the email address below.

Data we collect

None. Full Scale collects no personal data, no usage data, and no identifiers. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data. It requires no account and asks for no personal information. Apart from App Store purchase processing (below), the app makes no network connections.

Data stored on your device

Exercises, navaids, fixes, settings and imported charts that you create are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app permanently deletes them. There is nothing for us to retain, and nothing we could delete on your behalf — we never receive it.

Purchases

The optional Full Scale Pro unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy. The app checks your purchase entitlement with Apple's StoreKit framework on your device; no purchase information is sent to us.

Children

Full Scale is a professional training aid and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, CCPA and Thailand's PDPA grant rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Since Full Scale collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side, and no consent to withdraw. All of your content can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what is collected — for example, opt-in crash reporting — this policy will be updated at this URL before that version ships, with a new effective date.
