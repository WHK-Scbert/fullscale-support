Frequently asked

Is this a real navigation app?

No. Full Scale is a training aid only. Its stations, fixes and geometry are placeholders on a flat training grid, not survey data. Never use it for real-world navigation — always fly from current official publications.

What is included in the free version?

The full HSI and map sandbox — drag the aircraft, stations and fixes, add wind, fly intercepts — plus one exercise in each category: a radial intercept, a DME arc, and a holding entry.

What does Full Scale Pro unlock?

  • Every exercise in the library, plus everything added in future updates
  • Publishing your own holding patterns and DME arcs
  • Approach-chart PDF overlays on the map
  • Editing the navaid database — add navaids, fixes and marker beacons

Pro is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no account.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open any locked feature to bring up the unlock screen, then tap Restore purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple ID that bought Pro.

My imported chart disappeared after relaunch.

Known limitation: chart overlays are not yet saved between sessions. Re-import the PDF for now — persistence for charts is planned.

Privacy

Privacy policy

Full Scale collects no data. The app has no network connection, no analytics, no tracking, and no account. Everything you create stays on your device. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Since no data is collected, none is shared or sold.

Full Scale is an independent training aid. Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any aircraft manufacturer or air force.
